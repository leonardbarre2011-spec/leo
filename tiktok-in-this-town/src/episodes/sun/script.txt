In this town, the sun is controlled by one old man.
He lives on top of the hill, next to a giant light switch.
Every morning at seven. Click! Day.
Every evening at seven. Click! Night.
For eighty years, he never missed a single day.
Until last Tuesday.
Seven PM. No click.
Eight PM. Still sunny.
Midnight. The sun was still shining.
Nobody could sleep.
The mayor wore three sleeping masks.
My neighbor Gary was mowing his lawn at three AM.
The roosters were losing their minds.
So I climbed the hill.
The old man was fast asleep in his chair.
He was just too tired.
So I grabbed the giant switch and pulled as hard as I could.
Click! Night.
The whole town cheered.
The next morning, there was a letter on my door.
Congratulations. You are the new Switch Keeper.
Turns out, whoever touches the switch has to do it forever.
Now I set four alarms every single night.
And the old man?
He's finally on vacation. At the beach.
Complaining that it's too sunny.
